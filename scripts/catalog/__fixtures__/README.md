Minimal markup fixtures that reproduce the structure of the Tamiya pages the
parsers read. They are hand-written rather than saved copies of the live pages:
we only need the elements the selectors touch, and Tamiya's page content is
copyrighted (see CLAUDE.md, "Data and IP Rules"). The item numbers, prices and
dates in them are facts taken from the real pages.

Regenerate nothing here automatically — if a parser test fails after Tamiya
changes its markup, update the fixture by hand to match the new structure.
