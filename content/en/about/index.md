# About This Site

The Mini 4WD Beginner's Guide Information Site is dedicated to providing comprehensive and accurate Mini 4WD information to help newcomers quickly get started with this fascinating hobby.

## Site Goals

- Beginner-friendly getting-started guides
- A complete parts database
- Tuning and modification know-how
- Race classes and event information

## Contact Us

For any questions or suggestions, please contact us through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/williamchong/mini4wd-parts/issues).
