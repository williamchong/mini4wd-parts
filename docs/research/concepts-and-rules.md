## 1. Race classes and regulations

### 1.1 Tamiya official regulations (ミニ四駆公認競技会規則) — the "Open Class" baseline

Sources: [Tamiya EN regulation](https://www.tamiya.com/english/mini4wd/regulation.htm) (latest change: Dec 2025, Stock regulations added), [Tamiya JP regulation](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/regulation.html) (revised 2026-01-05), [Tamiya HK Chinese rule page](https://tamiya.hk/rule-stock-class/) (2026.5.12).

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Eligible product lines | Laser Mini 4WD, Mini 4WD REV, Mini 4WD PRO, Racer, Super, Fully-Cowled, Aero, Mighty, R/C Mini 4WD, Truckin' Mini 4WD |
| Max width | 105 mm (including rollers) |
| Max length | 165 mm |
| Max height | 70 mm |
| Min ground clearance | 1 mm |
| Min weight | 90 g, **including batteries and motor** |
| Tire diameter | 22–35 mm (front/rear common) |
| Tire width | 8–26 mm |
| Rollers | No limit on quantity (EN page) |
| Batteries | 2× AA, Tamiya-branded or FDK-made (JP/HK pages: タミヤ製・FDK製の単3電池2本) |
| Body | Must be painted or stickered and securely attached; home-made bodies prohibited |
| Parts | Tamiya Mini 4WD, R/C Mini 4WD (ラジ四駆) or Dangun Racer parts only |
| Jigs / setting boards | Not allowed in the race (JP page) |

**Allowed motors (Open Class)** — per the [Japan Cup 2026 page](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/japancup.html) and the EN regulation:
- ME / MA / MS (double-shaft PRO chassis): kit normal motor plus any Mini 4WD PRO motor (Torque-Tuned 2 PRO, Rev-Tuned 2 PRO, Atomic-Tuned 2 PRO, Light-Dash PRO, Hyper-Dash PRO, Mach-Dash PRO).
- All other (single-shaft) chassis: kit normal, Torque-Tuned 2, Rev-Tuned 2, Atomic-Tuned 2, Light-Dash, Hyper-Dash 3, Power-Dash, Sprint-Dash; older Torque/Rev/Atomic-Tuned (1), Hyper-Mini and Hyper-Dash 2 also still accepted.
- Ultra-Dash, Plasma-Dash (and discontinued Jet-Dash) are **not** allowed in official races ([MotorLab](https://motorlab-tw.github.io/en/benchmarks/tamiya-mini-4wd-motor-specs-list/), [monkeemods](https://monkeemods.com/tamiya/blog/tamiya-mini-4wd-race-classes-explained)).

**Japan Cup 2026 classes** ([Tamiya JP](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/japancup.html), [Japanese Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%83%9F%E3%83%8B%E5%9B%9B%E9%A7%86)):
- ジュニア Junior — elementary grades 4–6; Tune-series motors only (Torque/Rev/Atomic-Tuned 2 or their PRO versions; kit normal).
- ファミリー Family / トライアル Trial — grade 3 and below (Trial: paid, up to 8 runs/day, same motor list as Junior).
- オープン Open — any age; full Open motor list above; finals from 2nd run use supplied FDK batteries (J-CUP 2026 special).
- コンクールデレガンス Concours d'Elegance — judged on design, paint, detailing; also run online.
- Wikipedia also lists 限定クラス (Limited) and past チャンピオン classes. Japan Cup 2026 course: "UPBEAT MAELSTROM CIRCUIT 2026", ~270 m.

### 1.2 Tamiya Stock Class (ストッククラス / 基礎賽) — Tamiya's own bolt-on class

Sources: [Tamiya EN Stock Class](https://www.tamiya.com/english/mini4wd/regulation_stockclass.html) (effective 2026-05-11), [Tamiya JP](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/regulation_stockclass.html), [Tamiya HK 基礎賽 Stock Class 2026.5](https://tamiya.hk/rule-stock-class/), [mini4wd.tech explainer](https://www.mini4wd.tech/archives/mini4wd-stockclass-2026.html).

- Introduced April 2025 for Mini 4WD Station Challenge events; it is the official regulation with section 1-6 (Modifications) replaced.
- Same dimension/weight/battery/motor rules as Open (HK page: 105/165/70 mm, 90 g; single-shaft chassis may use Torque-Tuned 2, Rev-Tuned 2, Atomic-Tuned 2, Light-Dash, Hyper-Dash 3, Power-Dash, Sprint-Dash; MA/MS use PRO motors). I.e., **Dash motors are allowed**.
- **Banned**: cutting/shaving/hollowing the chassis, home-made chassis, new screw holes, cutting/shaving tires, modifying any non-body part, splitting the body, soldering or double terminals, non-standard battery orientation.
- **Allowed**: body hollowing and cutting for part clearance, countersinking existing holes on FRP/carbon plates and chassis, ball bearings, mixing tire/wheel sizes, cutting brake sponge, gluing shafts into gears, pushing shafts through wheels.
- **Forbidden zone (禁止エリア)**: between front and rear axles (rear half of front wheels to front half of rear wheels) only body and driver figure may be present.
- Parts that can touch the wall must be fixed rigidly to the chassis or to a plate fixed to the chassis; "plates may not be mounted so they wobble" (this kills 提灯/catcher-damper style gimmicks).

### 1.3 B-MAX GP (Basic-MAX GP, Bマックス)

Sources: [basic-max.com About](https://basic-max.com/about/), [ver4.0s (2025-12-17)](https://basic-max.com/2025/12/17/bmaxgp-regu-v4s/), [ver5.0 (2026-05-13, effective 2026-06-01)](https://basic-max.com/2026/05/13/bmaxgp-regu-v5/), [mini4wd.tech unification explainer](https://www.mini4wd.tech/archives/bmax-regulation.html), [Second Green Ivy Walls blog](https://www.secondgreenivywallsmini4wd.com/archives/post-16950.html), [Traditional-Chinese Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/%E8%BF%B7%E4%BD%A0%E5%9B%9B%E9%A9%85).

- **What**: a "semi bolt-on" (準ポン付け) regulation layered on top of Tamiya's official rules, restricting part processing and gimmicks to lower the entry barrier for beginners and returning racers. Originated in the Force Labo (Yokohama) community around 2019–2021; the Basic-MAX GP Executive Committee (within Sanaris Co., Yokohama) was formed June 2021 and manages the rules, the annual B-MAX GP 全日本選手権 (6th edition in 2026), and lists Tamiya as a cooperating company. Sponsors include EDION and Kyocera.
- **December 2025**: Tamiya's Stock Class and B-MAX GP regulations were unified (one machine legal in both); Stock Class races and "B-MAX Challenge" now share dates (4th Sunday of odd months). Revised again May/June 2026 (ver5.0) to be easier for beginners.
- **Core rules (ver5.0)**: 
  - Additional parts: Tamiya Mini 4WD / ラジ四駆 / Dangun parts only. Motors: all Tamiya-legal motors including Dash series (organizers may restrict). Batteries per Tamiya rules, correct orientation only.
  - Chassis: no hollowing, cutting, or self-made chassis; no new screw holes; allowed: countersinking existing holes, 2 mm hole enlargement / drilling through existing holes.
  - Body: plastic or polycarbonate; must use standard Tamiya body catches; only hollowing and cutting for interference; wings/driver figures optional (falling off no longer DQs, body loss does).
  - Rollers: only factory combinations (no bearing swaps, no profile/ring changes). Gears: specified pairings per chassis.
  - Plates must not wobble; slide dampers/mass dampers OK as designed; pendulum/提灯/catcher-type swinging dampers banned; motor disassembly banned.
  - Wear limits count as "processing": mass-damper hole wear ≥3 mm, tire or roller diameter change ≥1 mm.
  - Same forbidden zone as Stock Class.
- Popular outside Japan: Taiwan (TMU 台灣迷你四驅車聯盟 runs a B-MAX class and a 改裝組; 台灣田宮四驅車同好會 discusses B-MAX rule updates), Malaysia shop nights ("BMAX" ≈ no chassis cutting, Tamiya parts only), UK (B-MAX GP UK).

### 1.4 "B-Stock" — findings

Searched in English, Traditional Chinese (B-Stock 迷你四驅 / 香港 / 澳門) and Japanese (Bストック). **No standardized or officially named "B-Stock" class was found.** What exists:
- **Tamiya "Stock Class"** — Tamiya HK labels it 基礎賽 ("Basic race"), and it is sometimes called "Basic Stock" (the Chinese Wikipedia article calls it 「基礎 Stock Class」). "B-Stock" is most likely a colloquial contraction of "Basic Stock" / a blend of "B-MAX + Stock" now that the two rule sets are unified.
- **"Box Stock"** — common in the Philippines ([Lil's Hobby Center](https://www.lilshobbycenter.com.ph/pages/mini4wd-box-stock-rules-and-regulations-2025): single unmodified kit, white-cap 130 normal motor only, no tune-up parts), the UK ([mini4wd.uk](https://mini4wd.uk/mini-4wd-racing/box-stock-class-rules)) and Malaysia (meaning varies by shop, per monkeemods).
- **"B-Tuned"** — a US shop class (Downtown Hobby 9): Tune-series motors and plastic rollers only.
- Regional stock variants: "Stock (Thailand)" and "Stock Pro / xStock / Stock Advance Pro" (Philippines), Vietnam Stock Class 2026 ([tamiyavietnam.vn](https://tamiyavietnam.vn/eng-vie-tamiya-mini4wd-stock-class-regulation-in-vietnam-nam-2026/)).
- **Confirmed 2026-09-08 by the site owner: "B-Stock" means Stock Class.** The search findings above stand as the reason no external source defines it — it is local shorthand, not a published class name.
- Recommendation for the site: treat "B-Stock" as an alias entry that redirects to Stock Class / B-MAX, with a note that organizers' PDFs govern. monkeemods' advice: always check "which PDF, which motor list, which battery rule".

### 1.5 Other classes worth a glossary entry

- **Junior / Family / Trial / Open / Concours d'Elegance** — see 1.1.
- **Tamiya Mini 4WD Asia Challenge (TMAC)** — 2026 held in Manila, ~200 racers from 12 territories (incl. Hong Kong, Taiwan, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam, China); two classes: Stock and Open ([Tamiya TMAC 2026](https://www.tamiya.com/english/newstopics/2026/TMAC2026/index.html)). HK qualifiers (Hong Kong Cup 2026: PMQ, 合和, 荃灣廣場 stations) run 公開賽 Open Class and 學界賽/基礎賽 Stock Class ([tamiya.hk](https://tamiya.hk/product/hong-kong-cup-2026-pmq/)).
- **GT Advance (GTアドバンス)** — community "real-car" regulation: essentially unmodified chassis, real-car style plastic bodies, black tires only, plastic rollers max 8, no ball bearings, motors limited to Normal/Torque-2/Rev-2/Atomic-2/Light-Dash ([kakamigahara comparison](https://www.kakamigahara-bunka.jp/archives/2982), [GTアドバンス覚書](https://sites.google.com/view/gtadvanced3249/)).
- **Flat (フラット) vs 3D/立体** — course types rather than classes; flat courses are pure speed (Sprint/Power-Dash, 3.5:1), 3D is today's mainstream and needs brakes + mass dampers ([Tamiya setting guide](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/cms/mini4wdsettingguide_basic.html), [ミニ四駆改造マニュアル@wiki レーススタイル](https://w.atwiki.jp/mini_4wd/pages/203.html)).
- **"Tune-only"** — commonly the Junior list (Tune series only); also seen as "Tuned class" in the UK.
- **MSフレキ / ATB (1-axis AT bumper) / 提灯 / スライドダンパー** — these are Open-class *build techniques*, not classes: MSフレキ is cutting the MS chassis's three-piece joints into a suspension; 1軸ATB + フロント提灯 + キャッチャーダンパー is the standard 2025 Open build ([mini4-masters](https://mini4-masters.com/)). All of these are illegal in Stock/B-MAX because they need chassis cutting or wobbling plates. "ゼロ改" turned up only as "Zero chassis modification", not a class.

## 4. Part taxonomy: what a kit includes, what you need, GUP categories

**Stock kit contents** (from Tamiya kit pages / [mini4wdracing Box Stock](https://mini4wdracing.com/mini-4wd-racing-classes-explained/)): chassis, body + stickers, gears (one kit ratio, see table above), wheels + tires, 2 mm shafts, propeller shaft (single-shaft chassis), plastic rollers (4–6 depending on chassis), terminals, motor (usually the FA-130 "normal"; some kits/Starter Packs include a tuned motor), plastic bushings ("eyelets"). **Not included**: 2× AA batteries (mandatory to run) and any tools beyond a screwdriver need.

**Required to run**: kit + motor + 2 AA batteries. **Everything else is optional** (GUP).

**Tamiya Grade-Up Parts (GUP, グレードアップパーツ) categories** — from the [TEA-League GUP list](https://www.tea-league.com/mt/tea/archives/1990/01/post_720.html), [Tamiya GUP catalogue](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/products/list.html?genre_item=303010) (243 items) and the [Tamiya EN setup guide](https://www.tamiya.com/english/mini4wd/setupguide/setupguide.htm):
- Rollers: plastic (low-friction), aluminium, ball-bearing, 2-piece; diameters 8/9/11/12/13/17/19 mm (e.g. 15475 19 mm aluminium bearing rollers).
- Bearings: 620/520 ball bearings, "mini round-hole" bearing sets (15111), 6-mm bearings for wheels.
- Gears: setting gear sets giving 3.5:1 (超速), 3.7:1, 4:1, 4.2:1, 5:1; counter gears; lightweight/hollow propeller shafts (15073, 15234).
- Wheels: large/small diameter, narrow/wide, carbon-reinforced, aluminium, one-way (15022).
- Tires: soft, hard, super-hard, low-profile, barrel/arched, sponge (Reston).
- FRP / carbon plates: front/rear wide stays, multi-stays, side stays, HG carbon (many under 95xxx).
- Bumpers/brakes: FRP rear brake stay, brake sponge (grey/white/blue/black grades), skid bars.
- Dampers: mass dampers (side, ball-link, tail), slide dampers (15128/15129), stabilizers (stabi rollers 15027).
- Shafts: 60 mm / 72 mm, hollow stainless (~40% lighter), reinforced.
- Motor mounts / motor supports (15149), gold terminals (15045…), battery holders.
- Body catches, roller stoppers, screw/nut sets (stainless bis set 15508, aluminium lock nuts), tuning tools (pinion puller 15207, body-catch tool, speed checker), part cases (15163).
- Beginner bundles: **First Try Parts Set** (ファーストトライパーツセット, since 2011; per-chassis versions e.g. 15450 AR) with FRP plates, 19/13 mm rollers, stabilizers, mass dampers; **Starter Packs** (kit + parts, e.g. 18710 series).

**Item number scheme** (from Tamiya JP kit and parts lists: [kits 3010](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/products/list.html?genre_item=3010), [限定 303025](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/products/list.html?genre_item=303025), [特別企画 303020](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/products/list.html?genre_item=303020); JP Wikipedia confirms "GUP = 15000番台"):
- **18xxx** — regular Mini 4WD kits (Racer 18001 Hotshot Jr. onward; PRO/REV/Laser kits also in 186xx, e.g. 18660 Shteer MA, 18663 ME Racer).
- **19xxx** — Fully-Cowled and Aero Mini 4WD kits (e.g. 19445 Buster Sonic Premium, 19449 Tridagger WX on AR).
- **15xxx** — standard Grade-Up Parts, numbered "GP No.xxx" (15001 Hyper-Mini motor … 15550+ in 2026).
- **95xxx** — 限定販売 (limited) and 特別企画 (special project) items: J-CUP coloured motors, HG carbon plates (95514 HG carbon chassis base), HG aluminium bearing rollers (95404, 95709), limited kits (95190 Iron Burn J-CUP 2026, 95694 豊年 2026), Station-limited items.
- **94xxx** — older limited/special items (e.g. 94605 GUP Set Classic Vol.3), now mostly historical.
- "HG" (ハイグレード) is a name prefix used on premium carbon/aluminium GUPs, most of which are sold as 95xxx special-project items rather than catalogue 15xxx parts. "Hop-Up Options" (OP.xxxx, 5xxxx numbers) is Tamiya's **R/C** upgrade line and is not used for Mini 4WD ([Tamiya Hop-Up list](https://www.tamiya.com/english/products/list.html?genre_item=e_203020)).

## 5. Setup concepts for beginners

Sources: [Tamiya EN setup guide](https://www.tamiya.com/english/mini4wd/setupguide/setupguide.htm), [Tamiya JP setting guide](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/cms/mini4wdsettingguide_basic.html), [mini4wd.tech 7-step upgrade guide](https://www.mini4wd.tech/archives/mini4wd-recommendedparts.html), [rionch たからばこセッティング](https://rionch.com/2024/05/20/), [KATSU 立体セッティング](https://note.com/iinukawaingone/n/n2e0af9f28a09).

- **Gear ratio**: 5:1 = torque/acceleration (technical, uphill), 3.5:1 (超速) = top speed (flat, long straights); 4:1 / 4.2:1 / 3.7:1 in between. Best ratio depends on motor, weight and tire diameter, and must be from the chassis's matching list.
- **Rollers**: wider (up to 105 mm) runs the inside line and stabilises; ideal placement is equidistant from the tires, roughly 1 cm outside the tire centre line. Classic beginner layout: たからばこ (treasure-box) setting — 2 rollers rear, 1 front, i.e. 19 mm front + 13 mm rear stack. Bearing/aluminium rollers reduce friction; 6 rollers is the norm on modern chassis.
- **Tires**: hard/low-friction tires give more corner speed (rollers do the cornering); small-diameter (24–26 mm) tires are dominant on 3D courses for weight and low CoG; big diameter for flat speed.
- **Weight**: 90 g minimum; lighter = faster but jumpier. Clear polycarbonate bodies save weight (Stock/B-MAX now allow them).
- **Brakes**: sponge on front/rear under-stays, cut to height; controls speed into slopes/jumps. Grades (white/blue/black…) differ in grip.
- **Mass dampers**: absorb landing bounce; side/ball-link types are the legal Stock/B-MAX forms; swinging 提灯/catcher types are Open-only.
- **Flex**: MSフレキ (cut MS chassis) and ATB (pivoting AT bumpers) are the modern Open-class "standard 4-set" (MSフレキ + 1軸ATB + フロント提灯 + キャッチャーダンパー); not legal in Stock/B-MAX.
- **Front/rear balance**: front-motor (FM-A) is stable on up/down; rear-motor traction on flat; mid (MA/MS/ME) balanced.
- **Course types**: flat (speed decides; Sprint/Power-Dash, 3.5:1, big tires) vs 3D/立体 (mainstream; needs brakes + dampers, smaller tires, moderate motor).

**Beginner build archetypes** (synthesised from the above):
1. *Box/kit run* — kit + normal motor + batteries; add bearings only.
2. *First upgrade set* — Tune motor + First Try Parts Set (FRP stays, 19/13 mm rollers, stabilizers, mass dampers) = mini4wd.tech steps 1–2 and 5.
3. *Stock / B-MAX bolt-on build* — MA or VZ/AR with Light-Dash or Hyper-Dash 3, FRP/carbon wide stays, aluminium bearing rollers, brake stay + sponge, side mass dampers, polycarbonate body; no cutting, plates rigid.
4. *Flat-course speed* — Sprint-Dash/Power-Dash, 3.5:1, large hard tires, minimal dampers/brakes, light body.
5. *3D/technical stability* — Hyper-Dash 3 / Torque-Tuned 2, 4:1–4.2:1, small low-friction tires, front brakes, mass dampers, wide rollers.
6. *Open-class gimmick build* (advanced) — MSフレキ + ATB + 提灯 + catcher damper.
